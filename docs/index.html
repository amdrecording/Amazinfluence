Getting to your first commission

  1. Pick 3 products you own and genuinely use, ideally rated 4★+ with healthy sales — clean, mainstream products review well.
  2. Film 30–60 seconds each: hook, hands-on demo, honest take. Phone camera and window light are plenty.
  3. Upload through the Amazon app to your storefront, tag the exact product.
  4. Wait out the review (days to a few weeks). Log any rejection here — the patterns teach you the rules fast.
  5. 3 approved videos unlock onsite placement — your videos start appearing on product pages, where the real commissions are.

What to say, what to skip

Do

  • Hook in the first 3 seconds — name the problem or show the result.
  • Product physically in your hands, shown in use.
  • 2–3 specific observations beat ten generic ones.
  • Include one honest drawback — trust is what converts shoppers.
  • Close with who it's for (and who it's not for).
  • Disclose on offsite reposts: "#ad" / "I earn commissions" (FTC).

Don't

  • Mention price, discounts, deals, coupons or "on sale" — prices change; this is the #1 rejection reason.
  • Make health or medical claims ("cures", "treats", "clinically proven").
  • Name competitors or send viewers off Amazon.
  • Claim things you can't show on camera.
  • Use copyrighted music, profanity, or brand-bashing.
